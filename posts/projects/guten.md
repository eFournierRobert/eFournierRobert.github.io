# Guten
---
Codeberg Repo: [guten](https://codeberg.org/efournierrobert/guten)

Why I made Guten : [The joys of custom tooling](/posts/posts/guten.html)

---

Guten is a static website generator written in Go, designed to be simple to use and lightweight.
It is the generator used for this website.

The main thing I wanted to provide from Guten was a way to build a website with something smaller
and lighter than Jekyll, while keeping it to a single binary with no other dependencies.
