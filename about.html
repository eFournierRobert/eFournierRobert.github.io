About me

Growing up poking around the menus of various operating systems, from video game consoles to a random Windows Vista computer, quickly got me interested in computers and programming.

That curiosity led me to the field of system programming, low level software and operating systems internals. This website is where I can talk about all of those and share my projects.

I'm currently studying software engineering at École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS).
